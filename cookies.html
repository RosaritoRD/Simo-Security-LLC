Cookie Policy.

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Essential portal cookies

After successful authentication, the SIMO portal uses secure, HTTP-only cookies for short-lived access and renewable session tokens. These cookies are required to keep you signed in, refresh an authorized session, and protect restricted pages. They are not available to browser JavaScript.

Firebase and Google Sign-In

Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In may use browser storage, cookies, or request metadata while verifying identity. Their processing is controlled by Google and Firebase in addition to the SIMO application.

Public website

SIMO does not currently use advertising cookies or a behavioral advertising profile on this website. External font and icon providers may receive basic request information such as IP address, browser data, and the requested resource.

Managing cookies

You may block or clear cookies in your browser, but the secure portal will not function without its essential session cookies. Signing out clears the active SIMO session cookies and revokes the corresponding application session.

Changes

This page will be updated if analytics, preference storage, or other non-essential technologies are added.
