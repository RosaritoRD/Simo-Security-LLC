Accessibility

Access for Every Visitor.

SIMO Security is working to make its public website and secure portal understandable, navigable, and usable across devices and assistive technologies.

Our approach

The site uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, descriptive labels, alternative text, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support where practical.

Known limitations

Some third-party authentication interfaces and externally hosted resources are controlled by their providers. We continue to review our own pages as features evolve.

Request assistance

If you cannot access information or complete a portal task, contact us at (201) 312-3509 or support@simosecuritygroup.com. Tell us the page, device, browser, and assistance you need. Do not email passwords or sensitive identity documents.

Feedback

Accessibility feedback helps us improve. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information through an accessible alternative when possible.
