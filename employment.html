Security Professionals Who Represent the Standard.

Professional Conduct

Reliable attendance, appropriate presentation, respectful interaction, and responsible decision-making.

Clear Communication

Concise radio, written, supervisor, and client communication under routine and demanding conditions.

Situational Awareness

Attention to people, access, movement, changes, and the potential consequences of action or inaction.

Sound Judgment

Proportionate response, policy awareness, de-escalation, and timely escalation when conditions require it.

Documentation

Accurate, objective, and timely reporting that supports accountability and client communication.

Readiness

Current credentials when required, role-appropriate training, and willingness to follow assignment instructions.
