Our Standard

Security That Earns Trust Every Day.

SIMO Security & Consulting LLC supports executives, companies, venues, properties, and private clients across the NYC Metro Area. Our work begins with listening, continues through careful planning, and is measured by professional execution.

Every engagement is built around the real environment: the people involved, the property, timing, movement, communications, and appropriate escalation paths.
