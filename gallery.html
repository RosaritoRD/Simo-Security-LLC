Operational Discipline

Every Assignment Has a Defined Picture.

Before deployment, the team should understand the client, location, schedule, points of access, responsibilities, communications, and escalation paths. During the assignment, changes are identified and communicated. Afterward, relevant outcomes are documented and reviewed.

  • 01Clear scope and client priorities
  • 02Site, route, and schedule awareness
  • 03Defined posts, handoffs, and escalation
  • 04Authorized reporting through the client portal
